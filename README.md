Eddie's Library

A centralized place to collect and organize PDFs of books I like or plan to read. Not all of them are technology related

# Categories

Chess - I tend to prefer Chessable courses to reading physical chess books tbh. Not as much here, mainly the original book version of different Chessable courses

Programming - Anything that is about programming languages, software development, operating systems, cybersecurity, or practical computer skills ends up here

Computer Science - Theoretical CS, algorithms, and discrete math

Science/Engineering - Physical sciences or engineering materials. Physics, Chemistry, Electrical, etc.

Philosophy/Humanities: Anything about politics, social sciences, or philosophy ends up here. Opinion-heavy subjects

Mathematics - Math textbooks
